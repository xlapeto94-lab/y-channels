Privacy policy

Y Channels · effective 13 November 2026

What Y Channels is

Y Channels is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and reads their view statistics. Only its owner uses it.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Y Channels asks for these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload and schedule the owner's videos.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected and whether a video has been published.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, watch time and likes of the uploaded videos.

Where the data stays

The sign-in tokens and the statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Y Channels has no server and sends data only to Google's YouTube APIs. It does not sell, share or transfer any data, shows no ads and uses no analytics, tracking or cookies.

Y Channels' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

The data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Access can be removed at any time on the Google account permissions page.

YouTube and Google

Y Channels uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
