Y Channels

Y Channels is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels at scheduled times, and reads the view statistics of those videos.

It is not a public service: there are no customer accounts, no ads and no server. It is used only by its owner to manage their own channels.

Y Channels uses YouTube API Services. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
